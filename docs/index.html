The field barcode

Each stripe is one year, January to December. brown = bare soil, green = growing plants, grey = no clear satellite view. Hover to read a day.

1,000 possible 2026 seasons built from 30 years of corrected NASA weather, brought to today's warmth. Each square is 1 season in 100. Shown next to how often it really happened.
