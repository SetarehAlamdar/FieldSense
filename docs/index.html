How these odds are made

1,000 possible 2026 seasons, each built from one of 30 real years (1996–2025) of NASA POWER weather, corrected against Environment Canada stations and brought to today's warmth. Each card also shows how often the risk really happened in those 30 years.

Best choice under 2,000 different priorities

How to read this

We weighed the criteria in 2,000 random ways (soil first, simplicity first, and everything in between) and counted how often each rotation came out best. A high share means the choice barely depends on priorities.
