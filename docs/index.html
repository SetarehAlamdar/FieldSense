Try your own field

Click on your field on the satellite map (or paste its location from Google Maps). FieldSense finds the field at that point and shows its history from space, its soil cover, next season's weather from the nearest station, and which rotation and why. It runs in our web app in about 5–10 minutes, with no login. Nothing is stored or published.

Prefer to run it yourself? Open the Colab notebook (free NASA Earthdata account needed).

How these odds are made

1,000 possible seasons, each built from one of 30 real years (1996–2025) of NASA POWER weather, corrected against Environment Canada stations and brought to today's warmth (the 2026 level of the warming trend; one more year adds about 0.04 °C, far smaller than the difference between years). Each card also shows how often the risk really happened in those 30 years.

    How to read this

    We weighed the criteria in 2,000 random ways (soil first, simplicity first, and everything in between) and counted how often each rotation came out best. A high share means the choice barely depends on priorities.
